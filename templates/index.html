Hack Week STV Vote

Drag candidates from the pool into the ranking area to vote. Top of the list = first preference. You can rank as many or as few as you like.

How does STV work?

Votes will be processed with a STV (Single Transferable Vote) system, specifically Meek's method.

Essentially, the aim for each project is to reach a quota of first-preference votes.

This means that first-preference votes have the highest weight in determining the outcomes. Your lower preferences only matter if your higher-ranked projects are eliminated or already have enough votes to win.

Only rank projects you'd genuinely want to work on! If you wouldn't want to join a project, just leave it in the pool. Ranking a project last still helps it beat projects you didn’t rank, so don't rank something just for the sake of completeness.
